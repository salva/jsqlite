## Inference and decision for parent review

A per-prepare builder with stable resolved source identities, register/cursor/label allocation, a **consumed** destination/producer contract, and loop entry/continue/break and row-valid/null-row ownership is a reasonable starting seam. Wire it through real production callers rather than introducing an inert generic facade; maintain existing Mem, B-tree, comparison, program/VM, cleanup, unsupported-admission and public binding/row/reset foundations. In particular, WHERE-selected loops must continue to own cursor advance and exit; destination disposition must represent non-public-row consumers as well as top-level rows. This is a technical migration hypothesis for [[card:card-t]] to review, not policy approval or a claim that all branches already fit it.
