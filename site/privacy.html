Privacy

Updated 6 November 2026. This JSQLite website is maintained by Salvador Fandiño. Contact the maintainer (public issue; do not include private information).

Optional Google Analytics

Only after you select “Accept analytics”, the homepage loads Google Analytics 4, provided by Google, to measure visits, sessions, device/browser information and approximate location. Google receives network information including your IP address when your browser contacts its servers. This may involve processing outside your country. See Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google Analytics uses data.

We do not send SQL, query results, URL query strings, fragments or the referring URL to Analytics. Enhanced measurement and Google signals are disabled; advertising storage and personalization are denied. Analytics cookies (_ga and _ga_…) are scoped to /jsqlite/ on this host and configured with a 180-day lifetime, which Google may refresh during use.

Your accept/reject choice is stored locally in your browser for up to 180 days. Rejecting keeps Google Analytics unloaded. You can withdraw consent via the homepage’s Analytics settings button; rejecting then removes this project’s Analytics cookies and reloads the page to stop the loaded tag. Withdrawal does not erase data already received by Google. Clearing browser storage removes the remembered choice. If local storage is unavailable, the site asks again on the next visit.

Playground and hosting

SQL and results stay in the browser; the demo fetches its bundled database and runs locally. This privacy page does not load Analytics. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process request logs independently of analytics consent; see GitHub’s Privacy Statement.
